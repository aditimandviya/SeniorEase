SeniorEase Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

SeniorEase is a senior-friendly accessibility and assistance application designed to make commonly used smartphone functions easier to access.

This Privacy Policy explains how SeniorEase handles information and device permissions when you use the application.

1. Information and Permissions We May Access

Depending on the features you use, SeniorEase may request access to certain device features and information.

Location

SeniorEase may access your device's location to provide location-based features, such as navigation and assistance with finding nearby services.

Location information is used when required for these features.

Microphone and Voice Recognition

SeniorEase may use the microphone when you use voice-recognition features.

Audio may be processed by the voice-recognition functionality required to interpret your request. SeniorEase does not require you to create an account to use the application.

Files and Documents

SeniorEase may allow you to select or access files and documents on your device when you use features that require file selection.

SeniorEase only accesses files when you use a feature that requires such access.

Phone and Calling

SeniorEase may provide functionality for making phone calls or initiating calls to selected numbers, such as emergency or assistance contacts.

The actual telephone call is handled by the device's phone functionality and mobile network.

2. Third-Party Services

SeniorEase may open or interact with third-party applications and services, including services such as Google Maps and Uber, when you choose features that require navigation or transportation.

When you use a third-party service, that service may process information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

SeniorEase does not control how third-party applications process information after you leave SeniorEase.

3. Data Collection and Sharing

SeniorEase does not require users to create an account.

SeniorEase does not sell users' personal information.

The application does not contain advertising SDKs.

Some features may require information to be processed by the device, the operating system, or a third-party service in order to provide the requested functionality.

We do not intentionally collect personal information beyond what is necessary to provide the application's features.

4. Data Storage

SeniorEase is designed to minimize the collection and storage of user information.

Information accessed through device permissions is used to provide the relevant feature. Where information is processed by a third-party service, that service's own privacy policy and data-handling practices apply.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by SeniorEase. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

SeniorEase is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Choices

You can control SeniorEase's access to device permissions through your Android device settings.

You may revoke permissions such as location, microphone, or file access at any time. Some SeniorEase features may not function correctly if the required permission is disabled.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date on this page.
